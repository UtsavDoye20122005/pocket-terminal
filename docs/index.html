Pocket Terminal

Use your Mac's terminal from this phone: from anywhere, even with the lid closed. Phone and Mac connect directly and everything is end-to-end encrypted.

To pair: on your Mac run bash install.sh (or node pair.js), then scan the QR code with this phone's camera.

Connecting…

Tap to run. When there's no live terminal, it's sent as a command and the reply shows in Commands.

Each command runs once, on its own, in your home folder (max 10 min). If the Mac is asleep it runs at its next check-in.
